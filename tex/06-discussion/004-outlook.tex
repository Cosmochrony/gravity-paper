\subsection{Outlook}
  \label{subsec:outlook}

  The present framework establishes that a minimal spectral entropy
  functional produces, after renormalization,
  an infrared-dominant local Einstein response.

  Possible extensions include:

  \begin{itemize}
    \item inclusion of additional operator sectors,
    \item analysis of Lorentzian continuation,
    \item coupling to matter operators,
    \item exploration of regimes in which quadratic curvature terms dominate.
  \end{itemize}

  More generally, the result supports the view that geometric gravity
  may arise as an effective low-energy manifestation of spectral
  operator structure.
