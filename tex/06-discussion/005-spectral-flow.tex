\subsection{Remark on geometric flows and spectral monotonicity}
  \label{subsec:ricci-flow}

  The functional $S_\Pi$ depends on the full eigenvalue spectrum of the
  elliptic operator $A_g$.
  Geometric flows such as the Ricci flow are known to induce monotonic
  behavior in certain spectral quantities, including the lowest
  eigenvalues of the Laplacian.

  Although $S_\Pi$ is not identical to Perelman's entropy functional,
  its spectral definition suggests a possible structural relation between
  geometric evolution and spectral entropy variation.
  In particular, monotonic properties of low-lying eigenvalues under
  Ricci flow may influence the infrared sector of the renormalized
  spectral stress tensor.

  A detailed analysis of this relation lies beyond the scope of the
  present work.
