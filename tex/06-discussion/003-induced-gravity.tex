\subsection{Relation to induced gravity}
  \label{subsec:induced-gravity}

  The mechanism identified here is structurally identical to
  induced gravity in the sense of Sakharov.
  The gravitational coupling emerges from renormalization of the
  geometric sector.
  The spectral cutoff scale sets the magnitude of the induced
  Einstein--Hilbert term.

  No assumption of fundamental gravitational dynamics is made.
  The geometric response arises from operator-theoretic structure.
