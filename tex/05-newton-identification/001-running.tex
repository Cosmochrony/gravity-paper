\subsection{Running of the Einstein--Hilbert coefficient}
  \label{subsec:running}

  In four dimensions, the subtraction of the $a_2$ pole generates a
  renormalization-scale dependence in the Einstein--Hilbert coefficient.

  For the minimal scalar Laplacian with $a_2 = R/6$, the scale dependence
  of the induced coupling satisfies
  \begin{equation}
    \frac{d}{d\log \mu}
    \left(
      \frac{1}{16\pi G_N(\mu)}
    \right)
    =
    \frac{1}{(4\pi)^2}
    \frac{1}{6}.
  \end{equation}

  This relation follows directly from the residue of the $a_2$ pole in the
  zeta-regularized determinant.
  It expresses the standard induced-gravity running generated by a minimal
  scalar degree of freedom~\cite{BirrellDavies1982,ParkerToms2009}.

  The absolute normalization of $G_N$ depends on the ultraviolet completion
  or cutoff prescription.
  However, its parametric dependence is fixed by dimensional analysis.
